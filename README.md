## 🛰️ About Team Xiro Gravity

**Team Xiro Gravity** is a passionate, student-driven team from the **Aviation and Aerospace University of Bangladesh (Batch 2026)**, dedicated to advancing knowledge and innovation in the fields of **aeronautics and astronautics** — to rise beyond limits and touch the sky.

An elite repository of pioneering research and technical breakthroughs, this initiative pushes the boundaries of human knowledge from within one of Bangladesh's newest hubs for aerospace education.

We believe the future belongs to those who dare to explore. In an era of rapid aeronautical and astronautical revolution, we stand committed to **learning, building, and pushing boundaries** — from the science of flight to the complexity of air defense systems. We transform knowledge into action, and ideas into reality.
